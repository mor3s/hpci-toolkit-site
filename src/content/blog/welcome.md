The toolkit was never really about the code. It is a small vocabulary — three agents,
a handful of relationships, and the rituals composed from them — and the interesting
part is what people do with it. This is where that gets written down.

Expect notes on rituals worth trying, accounts of adapting the toolkit to new contexts,
questions that came up in a workshop, and the occasional longer reflection on attention,
plants, and machines. If you have built something with the toolkit and want to write
about it here, get in touch.
