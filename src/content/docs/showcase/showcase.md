*The same backbone, put to different ends.*

The toolkit fixes only a small vocabulary — three agents, six relationships, and rituals composed from them — and leaves open what any particular practice is for. The prototypes gathered here are applications of that vocabulary, each reinterpreting it toward a different purpose. They are presented not as finished products but as demonstrations of how far the same model can be adapted while remaining recognisably itself.
